* Mapping of evaporation section with stream nos:
* Solution leaves 323F004 and passes through TT-323014 and LV-323505 (stream 319) it then mixed with line from 335F004 (stream 331). The combined stream enters   323E010 in which the solution is heated with LP steam in the shell side. After heating the solution enters 323F010 to separate liquid and gas phase under vacuum.
PT-323204 measure pressure inside 323F010
* Liquid phase is discharged from bottom of 323F010 to urea solution tank 323D002 through (TT-323014) (stream 315)
* Gases are discharged under vacuum from the top of 323F010 through (stream 790) HV-323605, hand valve controlled by HIC-323605.
A step increase in HV-323605 opening will cause a step decrease in pressure inside 323F010 and shell side of 324E002 and vice versa.
* The urea solution is discharged from 323D002 using pump 323P003 to feed 324E001 tube side (stream 317)
* The solution is heated by LP steam in the shell side.
(LP steam = steam from 322D001)
* The heated solution enters 324F001 to separate liquid from gases.
* PT-324201 indicates pressure inside 324F001.
* Gases is discharged from the top of 324F001 under vacuum  to mix with the gas stream from HV-323605 (stream 705). Atmospheric air is used as vacuum break through PV-324202 (stream 784).
* The mixed gas line (stream 705) is then cooled in the shell side of 324E002 by cooling water in the tube side. The condensed gases is discharged from the bottom of the shell side of 324E002 (stream 719) to 328D003. The uncondensed gases are discharged from the top of 324E002 shell side (stream 706) to 324F002.
* PIC-324202 is on shell side of 324E002 indicates and control pressure inside it by open/close PV-324202.
* LP steam (stream 924), the driving force for 324F002, enters 324F002 through HV-329605 to drive uncondensed gases from 324E002. Open/close HV-329605 is controlled by HIC-329605. A step increase in HV-329605 cause step decrease in pressures inside 323F010 , 324F001 and 324E002 shell side and vice versa.
* Discharged gases from 324F002 then goes to 323C005 (stream 708).
* Solution discharged from 324F001 enters the bottom of 324E003 (tube side) stream 401. TIC-324001 is used to indicate and control the discharged solution temperature by cascading PIC-329203. PY-324201 calculates the urea concentration in the discharged solution by inserting PT-324201 and TIC-324001 in an equation to deduce the urea concentration
* The solution is heated inside 324E003 using MP steam in the shell side. (MP steam is steam from 329D009)
* The heated solution then enters the bottom of 324F003 to separate gases from liquid.
* PT-324204 indicates pressure inside 324F003.
* Gases are discharged from the top of 324F003 to the shell side of 324E005 (stream 709).
* Atmospheric air acts as vacuum breaker (stream 783) passes through PV-324203 and injected to the gas line from 324F003 to 324E005.
* Gases in the shell side of 324E005 is cooled by cooling water in the tube side.
* PIC-324203 indicates and controls pressure inside the shell side of 324E005 by open/close PV-324203.
* Condensed gases in bottom of shell side of 324E005 is discharged to 328D003 (stream 720).
* Uncondensed gases from top of shell side of 324E005 is discharged to 324F004 (stream 712).
* Discharge gases from 324F004 goes to shell side of 324E006 (stream 714).
* Gases in shell side of 324E006 is cooled by cooling water in the tube side.
* Condensed gases are discharged from the bottom of 324E006 to 328D003 (stream 721).
* Uncondensed gases from 324E006 is discharged to 324F005 (stream 715).
* Gases from 324F005 is discharged to the shell side of 324E007 (stream 717).
* Gases in shell side of 324E007 is cooled by cooling water in the tube side.
* Condensed gases in bottom of shell side of 324E007 is discharged to 328D003 (stream 759).
* Uncondensed gases are discharged to the atmosphere through the vent stack 328V001 (stream 722).
* HIC-329606 controls HV-329606 which supplies LP steam as driving force for the ejectors 324F004 (stream 927) and 324F005 (stream 929). 
* A step increase in HV-329606 cause step decrease in pressures inside 324F003 and 324E005 shell side and vice versa.
* TIC-324002 indicates and controls the temperature of the discharged solution from 324F003 by cascading PIC-329212. AY-324701 calculates the urea concentration in the discharged solution by inserting PT-324204 and TIC-324002 in an equation to deduce the urea concentration
