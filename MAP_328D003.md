All three compartments have openings between them. Compartment III is therefore a liquid surge
volume, not the previously inferred 112.2 m3 vapor-only bay. P&ID 104 places LI-328507 on physical
compartment I and LI-328508 on physical compartment III; no dedicated level indicator is drawn on
compartment II. The current zero-resistance reduced model gives the three bays one level fraction;
the approved openings alone do not establish instantaneous equalization in the plant.

## Absorber link and compartment II

322C001 receives stream 755 as its lower shower. Its bottom liquid is stream 756 to 323C005. The
atmospheric absorber closes exactly:


## Compartment III — accumulation baffle

No dedicated external PFD liquid stream is assigned to compartment III. Its inventory changes through
the approved openings as it buffers the combined imbalance of compartments I and II. At the model
density anchor of 992 kg/m3, the full inventories are 17856, 42656, and 425568 kg respectively.

Opening areas, elevations, and hydraulic loss coefficients remain unavailable. The simulator therefore
uses the parameter-free communicating-vessel limit after each process integration step:

```text
level fraction = (M1 + M2 + M3) / (M1,full + M2,full + M3,full)
Mi = level fraction * Mi,full
```

This redistribution conserves total mass. Internal sensible energy is also conserved by moving liquid
at its donor-compartment temperature. Separate process temperatures are retained for compartments I
and II; the model does not claim instant thermal homogenization across the baffles.
