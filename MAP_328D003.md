**Read the datasheet with care.** `323D003 Datasheets.md` states plainly that the P&IDs do *not*
delineate line-to-compartment assignments and that its own stream allocations are *deduced* by
matching thermodynamic properties, and that the three compartment volumes are an *engineering
derivation* (a 50/30/20 split of 561 m³), not documented capacities. Two of its deductions are
contradicted by the PFD and by other datasheets — flagged as D-1 and D-2 below.

## 4. Compartment III — vapour disengagement / vent

Per `323D003 Datasheets.md`: no piped liquid feed. Its inlet is the flash gas evolving off the
Comp-I and Comp-II liquid surfaces as sub-atmospheric condensate (0.1–0.3 bar a) lets down to the
tank's ~1 bar a, plus passivation N₂/O₂ migrating through the process.

| stream | service | kg/h | °C | bar a | composition | to |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 341 | Carb. Gas | 80 | 43 | 1.0 | N₂ 68.04 %, O₂ 17.53 %, NH₃ 5.05 %, H₂O 8.21 %, CO₂ 1.17 % | atmospheric absorber / vent header |

**Not modelled at all.** `341` appears in `main.py` only inside comments. See gap G-1.

## 5. Gaps and conflicts

### G-1 — Compartment III and the tank vent (stream 341) are absent
The model is a two-compartment tank. There is no vapour space, no flash-gas evolution off the
Comp-I/Comp-II surfaces, and no 80 kg/h vent. Consequences: the tank cannot over-pressure or pull a
vacuum; dissolved-gas flashing as 0.1–0.3 bar a condensate enters a 1 bar a tank is silently
ignored, so the NH₃/CO₂ that the PFD sends out in 341 stays in the liquid; and 112.20 m³ of the
vessel's 561 m³ is unrepresented. Emission telemetry for the tank does not exist.

### G-2 — no level controller on either compartment
`LI_328I` / `LI_328II` are published as indication only. Already tracked in `handoff.md`
("Sprint item 3a (#17) awaits a 328D003 level controller"). Comp-I level is the integral of a
34 t/h inflow against a 34 t/h outflow with no trim, so it drifts on any imbalance.

### G-3 — Comp-I inlet split is proportional, not per-condenser
`m_719/720/721/759` all scale on one shared `cond_ratio` (audit C2, interim). Their four distinct
temperatures (45 / 40 / 41 / 55 °C) therefore stay in design proportion. Resolves with audit C24,
which is itself blocked on C33.

### D-1 — datasheet says stream 718 leaves Comp II; the PFD says otherwise
`323D003 Datasheets.md` (Compartment II, Discharge) claims 718/718A/718B are pumped out of Comp II
by "Reflux Pumps 328P002A/B". Contradicted by `323E011 323D011 323P008 Datasheets.md:31`
("drawn by the Lean Carbamate Pump 323P008 A/B and discharged as Stream 718"), by PFD-22 (718 at
45 °C / 4.1 bar a / ρ 1065 — carbamate liquid, not the 56 °C / ρ 992 ammonia water of the Comp-I
header), and by the model, which sources 718 from 323D011. **The datasheet deduction is wrong; the
code is right.** Comp II's real discharge is 755.

### D-2 — datasheet assigns stream 722 to the Comp-III vent
PFD-21 and `Mapping of Evaporation Section.md:34` both put 722 (31 kg/h, 55 °C) as the 324E007
non-condensable vent to the 328V001 stack. The tank's own vent is 341. **Treat 722 as the 324E007
vent** (audit B9 covers its missing path to 328V001); the datasheet appears to have absorbed it into
the tank headspace by property-matching.

### D-3 — DCS snapshot temperatures do not match the design anchors
`322P002 322E006 322C001 Datasheets.md:13` records Comp III at 72.1 °C / 32.9 % level, Comp I at
29.8 % level, Comp II at 81.5 °C. The model anchors Comp I at 56 °C and Comp II at 44 °C (PFD).
Not necessarily a defect — the DCS trace is a different operating point — but the ~40 °C gap on
Comp II is large enough that the operating basis should be confirmed before the anchors are trusted
off-design.
