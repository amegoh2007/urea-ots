## Absorber link

322C001 receives stream 755 as its lower shower. Its bottom liquid is stream 756 to 323C005. The
atmospheric absorber then closes exactly:

```text
756 (33358) + 702 (440) + 708 (462) = 343 (34180) + 341 (80) kg/h
```

P&IDs 103/1 and 103/2 refine the aggregate PFD path: the 323C005 N4 liquid outlet reaches 328D003
through 328V001, rather than through a direct C005-to-tank pipe. Stream 341 is the **unabsorbed-gas /
stack boundary** associated with this absorber route. It is not a liquid feed or a dedicated
328D003 third-bay stream.

## Controls and model status

- P&ID 104 draws `LI-328507` on physical bay I and `LI-328508` on physical bay III. The operator map
  calls them `LT-328507/508`; neither source shows a level-control valve, so the model correctly
  publishes both as explicitly open-loop inventories.
- The four condenser returns are individual live tears with their own temperatures; the former shared
  `cond_ratio` split is retired.
- Stream 741 is a normally closed, mass-conservative diversion of the purified-condensate export.
- P&ID 104 confirms physical bay II and shows an N23/N24 hand-valved connection between bays III and
  II, but it supplies no bay capacities, normal valve state, or overflow/weir elevations. The
  secondary equipment summary's 561 m³ and 50/30/20 split remain engineering deductions, not a
  primary capacity allocation. No bay-II dynamic inventory is added on that basis.
