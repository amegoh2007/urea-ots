## Absorber link

322C001 receives stream 755 as its lower shower. Its bottom liquid is stream 756 to 323C005. The
atmospheric absorber then closes exactly:

```text
756 (33358) + 702 (440) + 708 (462) = 343 (34180) + 341 (80) kg/h
```

Stream 341 is the **323C005 unabsorbed-gas outlet to 328V001 / the synthesis vent stack**. It is not a
328D003 headspace vent. The prior map's Compartment-III allocation was a secondary-source deduction
contradicted by the supplied operating map and has been withdrawn.

## Controls and model status

- `LT-328507` and `LT-328508` are level indications. The supplied map identifies no level-control
  valve for either compartment, so the model publishes both as explicitly open-loop inventories.
- The four condenser returns are individual live tears with their own temperatures; the former shared
  `cond_ratio` split is retired.
- Stream 741 is a normally closed, mass-conservative diversion of the purified-condensate export.
- The secondary equipment summary's 561 m³ three-way volume split is an engineering deduction, not a
  primary documented capacity allocation. A third-compartment state must not be invented without an
  approved vessel drawing or P&ID that defines its volume, nozzles, and operating role.
